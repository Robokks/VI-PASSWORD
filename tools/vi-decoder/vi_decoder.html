LabVIEW RSRC · client-side

VI Decoder

Drop a LabVIEW .vi file to decode its structure in your browser — nothing is uploaded. Reads the RSRC blocks, version, password status, and front‑panel terminals, and draws a flow schematic of the terminals and detected function nodes.

Decodes structure reliably; the flow schematic is best‑effort. Exact node wiring needs full heap parsing (pylabview).
